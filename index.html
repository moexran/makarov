Someone I never saw coming

I remember thinking, "Wow... he's way too good for me." So I admired you from afar and convinced myself that someone like you would never notice someone like me.

How We Got Close

Everything changed on July 2026, during our community service trip to Semarang. I honestly thought you weren't interested in me at all. You always looked so calm and composed that I assumed I was just another person in the crowd. What I didn't know was that while I was completely oblivious... you had been quietly watching me all along.

iii

Our First Contact

Who knew a Kalimantan meme would change everything? Out of nowhere, you sent me a meme about my hometown, and somehow one conversation before I realized it, talking to you became my favorite part of the day.

iii

Our First Date

Our first date was watching Obsession together, and somehow, I don't think either of us expected it to be as memorable as it became. We laughed, talked, teased each other, and everything felt... effortless. We clicked almost instantly, and somewhere during that day, I realized I never wanted it to end. Looking back now, I think that was the moment I knew my heart was in trouble.

iv

Long Distance

Not long after we started dating, you left for Singapore to pursue your Master's. I'll never lie and say it wasn't heartbreaking. I already missed you before you even left. But at the same time, I couldn't have been prouder. Seeing you chase your dreams makes me admire you even more. Although the distance became very real, my love for you only continued to grow.
